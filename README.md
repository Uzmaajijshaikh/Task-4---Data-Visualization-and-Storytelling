Data Analytics Internship – Task 4 (Data Visualization and Storytelling)

This repository contains my submission for Task 4 of the Data Analytics Internship.

Dataset - Sample Superstore Dataset

Objective - The objective was to create meaningful visualizations and communicate trends, patterns, relationships, and business insights through a clear visual story.

Tools Used
1. Power BI Desktop
2. Sample Superstore Dataset
3. GitHub

Visual Story Components
1. Profit Overview
2. Sales and Profit Relationship
3. Discount and Profit Relationship
4. Category Profit Performance
5. Conclusion and Recommendation

Analysis Performed
1. Reviewed the cleaned Sample Superstore dataset
2. Created KPI cards for Total Sales, Total Profit, and Total Quantity
3. Compared total profit across product categories
4. Analyzed the relationship between Sales and Profit
5. Analyzed the relationship between Discount and Profit
6. Examined category-level profit performance
7. Summarized the main findings and recommendations

Visual Storytelling
The visual story was organized into five pages to create a clear narrative:
1. Profit Overview - Introduced the business question and provided an overview of profit performance
2. Sales and Profit - Examined whether higher sales always result in higher profit
3. Discount and Profit - Investigated the relationship between discount and profit
4. Category Performance - Compared profit performance across product categories
5. Conclusion and Recommendation - Summarized the key findings and recommendations

Key Findings
1. Technology generates the highest total profit among the three categories
2. Sales and Profit have a moderate positive relationship, but higher sales do not always result in higher profit
3. Higher discounts are generally associated with lower profit, although the relationship is not strong

Recommendations
1. Focus on profitable sales rather than sales volume alone
2. Review high-discount orders
3. Investigate lower-profit categories such as Furniture

Dataset Note - The cleaned Sample Superstore dataset does not contain a Date or Month field. Therefore, monthly trend analysis was not included in the visual story.

Files

Conclusion - The Sample Superstore dataset was transformed into a five-page visual story using Power BI. The analysis focused on profit performance and examined the relationships between Sales, Profit, Discount, and product categories. The project helped communicate analytical findings through a clear and simple business narrative.
